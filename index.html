Make it stronger

A few simple changes can make a password much harder to guess.

  • Use a longer passphrase made from several unrelated words.
  • Mix in uppercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Avoid names, dates, keyboard runs, and reused passwords.

✦ What makes a password strong?

Long, unique passwords are harder to guess. A passphrase with several unrelated words can be easier to remember and safer than a short word with a few substitutions.
